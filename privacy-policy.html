Privacy Policy — AI Arena: Prompt Fighter

Last updated: September 24, 2026

AI Arena: Prompt Fighter ("the App") is an educational game made for kids ages 6-12 to practice writing clear instructions for AI. This page explains what information the App collects and how it is used.

What we collect

The App does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information. It does not ask for a name, email address, birthdate, or location, and it does not require an account or sign-in.

Game progress

The App saves your level progress and star ratings only on your own device, using standard local app storage. This information never leaves your device, is never sent to us or to anyone else, and is deleted if you delete the App.

In-app purchases

The App offers one optional in-app purchase (the "Prompt Master Pack") that unlocks additional levels. Purchases are handled entirely by Apple's App Store using the payment method already on the purchaser's Apple ID; we never see or collect any payment, card, or billing information.

To know which purchases to unlock, the App uses RevenueCat, a third-party subscription management service, to validate purchase receipts with Apple and keep track of entitlements. RevenueCat receives an anonymous, device-generated identifier and purchase/transaction data needed to do this — never a name, email address, or other information identifying who is using the device. You can learn more about RevenueCat's own practices at revenuecat.com/privacy.

No ads, no third-party advertising, no tracking

The App contains no advertising and no analytics or tracking tools used to profile or target users. Purchase validation as described above is the only data that leaves the device, and it is never used for advertising.

Children's privacy

Because the App is intended for children, we designed it from the start to collect no personal information. In-app purchases are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store's own purchase flow (including any parental controls or purchase confirmations Apple requires), and the App itself never asks a child for personal information at any point.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted at this same web address with an updated date at the top.
